Frequently Asked Questions

What is Computer Science?

Computer Science is the study of computers, programming, algorithms, software and information systems.

What courses are offered in Computer Science?

Courses include programming, databases, networking, web development, algorithms and software engineering.

What careers can Computer Science students pursue?

Graduates can work as software developers, system analysts, database administrators, network administrators and web developers.

Do Computer Science students study Mathematics?

Yes. Mathematics and logical reasoning are important in many areas of Computer Science.

Can I become a software developer?

Yes. Software development is one of the major career paths available to Computer Science graduates.
