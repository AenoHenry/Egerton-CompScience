Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education

Published: 10 September 2026

Artificial Intelligence is transforming higher education by providing new ways of teaching, learning and conducting research.

Universities are using AI tools to support students, improve research and automate some administrative activities.

Benefits of Artificial Intelligence

  • Personalized learning
  • Improved research
  • Faster access to information
  • Automated administrative tasks
  • Improved learning support

Challenges of AI

Despite its benefits, artificial intelligence also creates challenges such as academic integrity, privacy, misinformation and responsible use of technology.
